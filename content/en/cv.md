# What kind of engineer I am

- I do backend, frontend, infrastructure, requirements, client conversations, hiring and team leadership.
- I work best in small teams where the engineer also talks to customers, makes the call, and ships.
- I am product-oriented and try to understand the business side while building.

## Technology is a means

- I am more interested in **how people think, pay attention and use language** than in technology itself. At university I was drawn to behavioural economics, and my seminar was run by the novelist Shu Fujisawa.
- I am not a technology enthusiast. I have little interest in chasing the newest tools, and I learn what I need, when I need it, for what I am trying to build.
- So rather than specialising narrowly in technology, I want to **use technology in domains that deal with people**.

## Bottom-up learner

- I am not an academic type. Reading a textbook front to back to get the whole picture does not work for me.
- I start from what I want to build, learn only the parts I need, and gradually acquire the whole picture from that accumulation.
- I do not stop at "it seems to work". I repeat until I can honestly say I have got it.

## I design the environment, not the willpower

- I do not trust willpower. When I need to concentrate I put my phone somewhere physically out of reach, and solve it on the environment side.
- I habitually extract principles from experience and apply them to other domains.

# Personality

## Basics

- An introverted thinker, but active when it comes to working with people.
- I tend to notice small changes around me.
- I keep to "get one thing stronger each day". Growth itself is what motivates me.
- Once I commit, the sharper the situation, the deeper I can focus.

## What I am not good at

- Running several things in parallel. If I narrow to one thing I can go deep, but switching costs me a lot.
- Exhaustive, textbook-style learning. Trying to see the whole picture first tends to freeze me.
- Losing context to notifications. That is exactly why I build environments where notifications cannot reach me.
